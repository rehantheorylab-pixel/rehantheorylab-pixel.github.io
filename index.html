HOW I WORK

Measure, then claim.Every number carries the machine it ran on and whether it was measured or projected. A ratio computed from two estimates is labelled an estimate.

Credit upstream.A fork says what it is forked from in the first line. Attribution is a licence obligation under GPL, and a credibility requirement under everything else.

State the limit.If it isn't tested, the README says so before the reader finds out. Limitations sections are cheaper than retractions.
